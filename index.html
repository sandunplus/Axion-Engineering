Services

From surface to specification.

One team carries the object through design, engineering, and the pictures used to judge it.

01

Product design

Form, proportion, and the materials a person actually touches. We shape the object, then carry those surfaces into a production model.

02

Visualization

Studio lighting, material studies, and short films for reviews and launch. The image is checked against the model, so the promise matches the part.

03

Mechanical engineering

Mechanisms, structure, fasteners, and the room parts need to move. Assemblies are prepared with the design still intact.

04

Prototypes

A physical check before the expensive one. We coordinate prints, soft tooling, and the model changes that follow a fitting.

05

Spatial visualization

Buildings and interiors studied as geometry and light. Massing, materials, and views for decisions that have to survive the site.

Process

Five steps, one model.

Each phase ends with something you can judge: a direction, a file, a picture, or a part.

  1. 01 Listen

    Constraints, users, and how the object has to be made.

  2. 02 Form

    Sketches, massing, and a direction clear enough to choose.

  3. 03 Model

    Surfaces and engineering developed in the same file.

  4. 04 Prove

    Pictures, prototypes, and a review with the real issues visible.

  5. 05 Release

    Drawings, CAD, and a handoff a factory can use.
